Student Privacy and Data Use

This page explains what student information is, and is not, required to use Science Studio.

Will students be required to enter personal information?

No.

Students are not required to enter personal information to use this website. Science Studio does not require student accounts, usernames, email addresses, passwords, birthdates, home addresses, phone numbers, or student ID numbers.

What students can access without entering personal information

✅Lessons
✅Interactive labs and simulations
✅Vocabulary posters
✅STAAR-style review activities
✅Printable resources and foldables
✅Student dashboard and teacher dashboard

Optional fields

Some printable or reflection activities may include an optional field for initials or class number only. Students can also leave the field blank.

Students should not type full names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, birthdays, or student ID numbers into the website.

Storage and student work

This version of Science Studio does not use student accounts and does not save student work to an online classroom database.

Some interactive pages may temporarily save progress in the student's own browser using local device/browser storage so the activity can display a printable result or reflection. This information is not submitted to a teacher database by the app.

Teacher direction for students

Teachers should instruct students to use:

  • initials only,
  • a class number, or
  • leave the field blank.

Students should not enter personal identifying information.
