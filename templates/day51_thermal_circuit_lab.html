Day 51 Interactive Lab

Thermal Circuit Investigation

Trace electrical energy through a virtual heating circuit and use temperature evidence to identify the energy transformation.

Mission

Steve is testing a battery-powered warming system. Inspect the battery, switch, wires, heating element, and thermometer. Explain why the temperature increases when the circuit is complete.

🧪 This is a virtual investigation. Students should not intentionally heat wires or electrical components.

Energy Path

  1. Battery provides electrical energy.
  2. Closed switch completes the circuit.
  3. Wires carry electrical energy.
  4. Heating element transforms electrical energy.
  5. Thermal energy increases the temperature.

Temperature Evidence Challenge

A virtual circuit is turned on for two minutes. The temperature near the heating element changes from 22°C to 34°C.

What is the best conclusion?

Correct answer: A. The increase from 22°C to 34°C is evidence that thermal energy was produced. Electrical energy in the circuit transformed into thermal energy at the heating element.

Lab CER

Claim: Electrical energy can transform into thermal energy in a complete circuit.

Evidence: The temperature near the heating element increased after electrical energy flowed through the complete circuit.

Reasoning: The heating element transforms electrical energy into thermal energy. A rise in temperature provides measurable evidence of the transformation.
