🤝 Guided Practice — Paper Sedimentary Rock Model

Give students four differently colored paper strips.
  1. Stack the strips: Deposition.
  2. Press the stack between both hands: Compaction.
  3. Ask: Did squeezing alone model cementation?
  4. Draw dots or lines between the layers representing mineral cement: Cementation.
Ask students to explain: What can this model show? What can it NOT show?
