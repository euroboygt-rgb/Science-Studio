🔔 Bell Ringer — One Cause, Many Effects

Earth rotates once in about 24 hours.

List as many daily patterns as you can that are connected to Earth's rotation.

👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — The Earth Rotation Chain

🌎

EARTH ROTATES

West → East

About one rotation every 24 hours
☀️🌙

DAY & NIGHT

Locations rotate into and out of sunlight.

🌅☀️🌇

APPARENT SUN MOTION

The Sun appears to move east → west.

⬛

SHADOW DIRECTION

Shadows point generally away from the Sun.

📏

SHADOW LENGTH

Lower Sun = longer shadow. Higher Sun = shorter shadow.

🚨 Mission Control Rule

Do not memorize these as separate facts.

Ask: What is Earth doing?

Earth's rotation is the common cause connecting the patterns.
