Troubleshooting Checklist

  1. Is there a power source?
  2. Is the switch closed?
  3. Are all connections touching?
  4. Is the path made of conductors?
  5. Is there a complete path back to the battery?
  6. What energy output should occur?

Final CER

Claim: A functioning electrical system needs a complete conducting circuit.

Evidence: Systems fail when there is an open switch, disconnected wire, or insulator in the path. They work after the conducting path is repaired.

Reasoning: Electrical energy must travel through a complete conducting path. When the circuit works, electrical energy can transform into light, motion, sound, or thermal energy.
