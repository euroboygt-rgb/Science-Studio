👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — The STAAR Evidence Method

1️⃣

READ

What is happening in the scenario?

2️⃣

FIND EVIDENCE

What observation, model, direction, or data is given?

3️⃣

CONNECT

Which Earth-rotation idea explains the evidence?

4️⃣

ELIMINATE

Remove answers that do not match all evidence.

5️⃣

PROVE

Explain why your answer fits the evidence.
