🚨 Important Misconception

Day and night do NOT happen because the Sun travels around Earth every 24 hours.

The Sun stays fixed in our model.

EARTH ROTATES. That rotation moves locations into and out of sunlight.

📓 Science Notebook — Day & Night Model

Draw:
  1. Sun on the left.
  2. Earth on the right.
  3. Shade the half facing the Sun and label it DAY.
  4. Shade the half facing away and label it NIGHT.
  5. Draw a curved rotation arrow.
Write:

Earth rotates once in approximately 24 hours.

Day → Sunset → Night → Sunrise → Day

🤝 Guided Practice — Flashlight + Globe Model

Darken the room if possible.
  1. Hold a flashlight still to represent the Sun.
  2. Shine it on a globe.
  3. Identify the illuminated half.
  4. Identify the dark half.
  5. Place a sticky dot on one location.
  6. Slowly rotate the globe.
  7. Watch the location move: day → sunset → night → sunrise → day.
Ask: What moved — the Sun, or the Earth?
