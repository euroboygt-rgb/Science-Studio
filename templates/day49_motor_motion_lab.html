Energy Path

  1. Battery provides electrical energy.
  2. Closed switch completes the path.
  3. Wires carry electrical energy.
  4. Motor changes electrical energy into motion.
  5. Fan blade spinning is evidence of motion energy.

Motor Motion Challenge

Which build will make the fan spin?

Correct answer: B. The fan spins because the circuit has a battery, conducting wires, a motor, and a complete closed path. The motor transforms electrical energy into motion.

Lab CER

Claim: Electrical energy can transform into motion energy in a complete circuit.

Evidence: The fan spins only when the battery, wires, switch, and motor make a complete path.

Reasoning: Electrical energy flows through the circuit to the motor. The motor changes electrical energy into motion energy, causing the fan blade to spin.
