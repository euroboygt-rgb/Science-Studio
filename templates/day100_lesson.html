🤝 Guided Practice — Human Canyon Carver

Give several students paper "rock" pieces.
  1. Students tear or separate pieces: WEATHERING = BREAK.
  2. A student acting as the river moves the pieces downstream: EROSION = MOVE.
  3. Repeat several rounds.
  4. Widen the empty space each round to represent long-term canyon change.
Ask: "Did we build material here, or remove material?"

Expected: REMOVE — EROSION.
