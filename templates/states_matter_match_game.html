Day 6 Matter Solids • Liquids • Gases

🧊 Matter Changes How It Acts

Matter can be a solid, liquid, or gas. Each state has different properties because the particles are arranged and moving differently.

Part 1: Review the Three States

🧊

Solid

Has a definite shape and definite volume.

Particles packed together Particles vibrate
💧

Liquid

Has a definite volume but takes the shape of its container.

Particles close together Particles slide past each other
💨

Gas

Does not have a definite shape or definite volume.

Particles spread apart Particles move freely
