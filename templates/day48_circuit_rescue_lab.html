Mission

Steve has three circuit builds. Your job is to inspect the battery, wires, switch, bulb, and material in the path to decide whether electrical energy can flow through a complete circuit.

Circuit Requirements

  1. Power source, such as a battery
  2. Conducting path, such as wires
  3. Energy receiver, such as a bulb
  4. Closed path with no gaps

Circuit Rescue Challenge

Choose the build that will light the bulb.

Correct answer: B. The bulb lights because the circuit has a battery, conducting wires, a bulb, and a complete closed path. The open switch in A creates a gap. The string in C is an insulator, so electrical energy does not flow easily.

Lab CER

Claim: A bulb lights only when electrical energy can flow through a complete circuit.

Evidence: The working build has a battery, wires, a closed switch, and a bulb connected in one complete path.

Reasoning: Electrical energy needs a closed conducting path. If there is a gap or an insulator in the path, the circuit is incomplete and the bulb will not light.
