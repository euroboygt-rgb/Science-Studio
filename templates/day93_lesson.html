🕰️ Time Machine Mystery

Ancient Life Buried Deep Underground

❓ Mystery Question: How can material from organisms that lived millions of years ago eventually become a fossil fuel?
🚨 Critical Difference

Fossil ≠ Fossil Fuel

🦴

FOSSIL

Preserved evidence of an organism from the past.

≠
⛽

FOSSIL FUEL

An energy resource formed from ancient organic material under suitable conditions.

A visible fossil does NOT simply turn into gasoline.

🪨 W.E.D.C.C. Connection

Where Does W.E.D.C.C. Fit?

🪨 Sedimentary Rock

W → E → D → C → C

Weathering → Erosion → Deposition → Compaction → Cementation

⛽ Fossil Fuel

Ancient organic material → Burial → Long Time → Heat + Pressure

Deposition can help bury organic material, but cementation is not what creates fossil fuel.

STAGE 3

Deeper Burial

STAGE 4

🚨 STAAR Trap

Fossil fuels do NOT form in days, weeks, or a human lifetime.

They require extremely long periods of geologic time.
