🌊 Location Matters

What Is a River Mouth?

💧💧💧 RIVER ➡️ ➡️ ➡️
📍 RIVER MOUTH
🌊 🌊 🌊 LARGER BODY OF WATER
A river mouth is where a river enters another body of water.

In our model, when the river enters the larger body of water, its flow slows and some transported sediment can settle.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Follow the Sediment

A river carries a grain of sand downstream.

Answer:
  1. Is the sand moving or being deposited while it travels?
  2. What could happen when the river slows?
  3. Which process could then occur?

🤝 Guided Practice — Human Delta Builder

Choose one side of the room as the river and another as the lake/ocean.

Give several students paper sediment cards.
  1. Teacher says FAST RIVER: students carry sediment downstream.
  2. Teacher says RIVER MOUTH.
  3. Teacher says WATER SLOWS: students place sediment on the floor.
  4. Repeat several times.
Ask: "What landform is our deposited sediment building?"
