🧊 Don't Forget Ice

Moving Ice Changes Earth's Surface Too

🏔️ 🧊🧊🧊 ⬇️

A large moving body of ice is called a glacier.

As ice moves, it can scrape, loosen, carry, and transport rock and sediment.

When ice melts, some transported material can be deposited.

ICE IS AN AGENT OF CHANGE.

For Day 98, the important idea is that moving ice can erode, transport, and deposit Earth material.

👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — Agent → Process → Landform

💧

WATER

Can weather, erode, transport, and deposit sediment.

Canyon: erosion
Delta: deposition
💨

WIND

Can pick up and transport small particles.

Sand dune: deposition
🧊

ICE

Moving ice can scrape, erode, and transport rock and sediment.

Melting ice can also deposit sediment.
🚨 Key STAAR Distinction

EROSION = sediment is moving away.
DEPOSITION = sediment is being dropped.
