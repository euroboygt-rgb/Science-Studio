🌈 Phenomenon Mission

What Happens Inside a Raindrop?

Day 69 • Rainbow Science
❓ Mystery Question: How can white sunlight enter a raindrop and leave as different colors?
1️⃣ Refract In

Sunlight changes direction as it moves from air into water.

2️⃣ Reflect Inside

The light reflects from an inside surface of the droplet.

3️⃣ Refract Out

Light changes direction again as it moves from water back into air.

4️⃣ Spectrum

The visible colors leave along slightly different paths.

👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — Building a Rainbow

☀️ White Light

Sunlight contains many visible colors traveling together.

↘️ Refraction

Light changes direction when it crosses between air and water.

🪞 Internal Reflection

Light reflects from an inside surface of the raindrop.

🌈 Dispersion

White light separates into visible colors.

Rainbow Sequence

☀️ WHITE SUNLIGHT → ↘️ REFRACT IN → 🪞 REFLECT INSIDE → ↗️ REFRACT OUT → 🌈 SPECTRUM
