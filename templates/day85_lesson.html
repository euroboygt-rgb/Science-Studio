👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — The Full Weather Engine

☀️ SUN ENERGY

Provides energy that warms Earth's surface.

→
🌊 OCEAN

Stores enormous amounts of liquid water.

→
⬆️ EVAPORATION

Transfers water into the atmosphere.

→
☁️ CONDENSATION

Cooling can produce cloud droplets.

→
🌧️ WEATHER

Clouds and precipitation may develop.

🌦️ Systems Rule

Changing one part of an Earth system can influence another part.

More evaporation → more atmospheric moisture available

BUT:

More moisture ≠ guaranteed rain. Cooling, condensation, particle growth, and other atmospheric conditions also matter.
