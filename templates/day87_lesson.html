🗂️ Example Case

☀️ High solar energy 🌊 Ocean water ↓ 💨 Atmospheric moisture ↑

Process: Evaporation

Evidence: Water decreases at the surface while water vapor increases in the atmosphere.

Connection: That atmospheric moisture can later contribute to condensation and cloud formation.

🚨 Evidence Rule

Naming the correct process is only part of the job.

A strong scientist says: "I know because the evidence shows..."

📓 Science Notebook — Case File Evidence Table

Observation Likely Process Evidence System Connection

Moist air cools, clouds ↑ Condensation Water vapor changes into droplets Builds clouds

☀️ Sun → 🌊 Ocean → ⬆️ Evaporation → 💨 Water Vapor → 💧 Condensation → ☁️ Clouds → 🌧️ Possible Precipitation

🤝 Guided Practice — Reveal One Clue at a Time

Give students this case:

"Something changed in the atmosphere."

Reveal clues one at a time:
  1. Atmospheric moisture is high.
  2. Air temperature decreases.
  3. Cloud cover increases.
After each clue ask: What can we conclude now? What can we NOT conclude yet?

Final target: students identify condensation and defend it using moisture + cooling + clouds.
