👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — From Swamp to Coal

1
🌿🌳🌿

Ancient Swamp

Large amounts of plant material grow in wet environments.

2
🍂🍂🍂

Plant Material Accumulates

Dead plant material builds up in the wet environment.

3
🟫

Peat Forms

Accumulated partly decayed plant material forms peat.

4
⬇️🥞🥞

Burial

Sediment and rock accumulate above the peat.

5
♨️🗜️🕰️

Coalification

Heat, pressure, and geologic time change the buried material.

6
⚫

Coal

A solid fossil fuel remains underground.

🚨 Important

Peat does not become coal simply because sediment touches it.

Coal formation requires burial + changing heat and pressure conditions + extremely long geologic time.
