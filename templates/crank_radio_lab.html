Lab Mission

A wind-up emergency radio uses a crank, gears, generator, wires, speaker, light, and warm components. Your job is to trace the energy path through the system.

Energy Path Checklist

  1. Mechanical energy starts with the crank.
  2. Gears transfer mechanical energy.
  3. The generator changes mechanical energy into electrical energy.
  4. Electrical energy flows through the circuit.
  5. Electrical energy changes into sound, light, and thermal energy.

Lab CER

Claim: A crank emergency radio transforms energy through a system.

Evidence: The crank moves, gears spin, the generator produces electrical energy, the speaker makes sound, the lamp gives off light, and some parts warm up.

Reasoning: Mechanical energy can transform into electrical energy, and electrical energy can transform into sound, light, and thermal energy.
