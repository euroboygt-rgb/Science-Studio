🎯 Learning Target

  1. List as many rainbow colors as you can remember.

👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — Meet ROYGBIV

R Red
O Orange
Y Yellow
G Green
B Blue
I Indigo
V Violet
Important:

The prism does not create the colors.

The colors were already part of the white light.

📓 Science Notebook — Build the Spectrum

Write ROYGBIV vertically:

R — Red
O — Orange
Y — Yellow
G — Green
B — Blue
I — Indigo
V — Violet

Draw white light entering a prism and the seven visible colors leaving it.
