🕵️ Weather Detective Method

1️⃣ OBSERVE → 2️⃣ COMPARE → 3️⃣ FIND PATTERN → 4️⃣ FORECAST → 5️⃣ DEFEND WITH EVIDENCE

📊 Example Pattern

Across five days:

  • temperature decreases,
  • cloud cover increases,
  • precipitation increases.
A reasonable forecast:

Cooler, cloudy conditions with precipitation still possible.
🚨 Scientist Language

A forecast is a prediction supported by evidence.

It is not a guarantee.

Say: "The data suggest..." or "The evidence supports..." instead of "This definitely will happen."
