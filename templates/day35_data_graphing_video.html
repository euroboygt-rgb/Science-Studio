Teacher Talk Track

  1. Question: How does ramp height affect the distance a toy car travels?
  2. Independent variable: ramp height, because that is what we change on purpose.
  3. Dependent variable: distance traveled, because that is what we measure.
  4. Controlled variables: same car, same surface, same starting line, same measuring tool.
  5. Data: collect repeated trials and organize the results in a table.
  6. Graph: x-axis shows ramp height; y-axis shows distance traveled.
  7. Claim: The toy car traveled farther when ramp height increased.
