Identify the system described by each clue:
  1. Minerals bind sediment grains.
  2. A fish fossil is discovered beneath a desert.
  3. Ancient plants form peat.
  4. Tiny aquatic organisms become deeply buried.
  5. The lowest layer in an undisturbed rock column.
