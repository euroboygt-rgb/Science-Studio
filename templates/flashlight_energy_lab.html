Energy Path

  1. Battery stores chemical energy.
  2. Closed circuit allows electrical energy to flow.
  3. Bulb transforms electrical energy.
  4. Outputs are light energy and thermal energy.

Lab CER

Claim: A flashlight transforms energy when the circuit is complete.

Evidence: The battery, wires, switch, and bulb work together; the bulb shines and may get warm.

Reasoning: Chemical energy in the battery changes to electrical energy in the circuit, then electrical energy changes into light and thermal energy in the bulb.
