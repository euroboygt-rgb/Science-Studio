👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — The Atmosphere Changes the Particle

🌧️

RAIN

Liquid water reaches the ground without refreezing.

❄️

SNOW

Frozen crystals remain frozen through sufficiently cold air.

🧊

SLEET

Frozen precipitation melts in a warmer layer, then refreezes before reaching the ground.

⚪

HAIL

Ice grows inside strong thunderstorms with powerful updrafts.

🌡️ Read the Atmosphere From Top to Bottom

CLOUD LEVEL Cold air
MIDDLE LAYER May be warm or cold
SURFACE LAYER May be warm or cold

Follow the particle through every layer. Its state can change before it reaches the ground.

⛈️ Hail Is Different

Hail is not explained simply by a cold layer near the ground.

Inside a strong thunderstorm, powerful updrafts can carry ice upward repeatedly. The hailstone can collect more supercooled water and grow in layers.

Strong thunderstorm + strong updraft = hail-building conditions
🚨 Model Warning

Real atmospheric conditions are more complex than our three-layer classroom model.

We are using simplified profiles to practice evidence-based predictions.
