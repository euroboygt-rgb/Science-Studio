🚨 Two Important Differences

EROSION

Sediment is MOVING.

DEPOSITION

Sediment is DROPPING / SETTLING.

COMPACTION

Sediments are SQUEEZED.

CEMENTATION

Minerals BIND the grains.

🤝 Guided Practice — W.E.D.C.C. Hand Motions

Students stand.

Call each letter and perform the motion:
  • W — Weathering: hands together, then break apart.
  • E — Erosion: slide both hands sideways.
  • D — Deposition: lower hands and place them down.
  • C — Compaction: press palms toward each other.
  • C — Cementation: lock fingers together.
Repeat: slowly → normal speed → fast → from memory.
