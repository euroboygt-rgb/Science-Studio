👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — Earth's Two Motions

NORTH POLE
🌎
SOUTH POLE

Earth's Axis

Earth's axis is an imaginary line through Earth from the North Pole to the South Pole.

Earth spins around this axis.

🔄

ROTATION

Earth spins on its axis.

🕛 ≈ 24 HOURS
Think: SPIN
☀️🌎

REVOLUTION

Earth travels around the Sun.

📅 ≈ 1 YEAR
Think: ORBIT

🤯 Earth Does Both at the Same Time

🔄 Earth rotates while ☀️🌎 Earth revolves around the Sun.
🚨 Do Not Mix These Up

ROTATION = SPIN
Earth spins around its axis.

REVOLUTION = TRAVEL AROUND
Earth travels around the Sun.
