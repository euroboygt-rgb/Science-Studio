Mission

Steve needs an emergency alarm. Inspect the battery, switch, wires, speaker, and sound waves. Explain how electrical energy transforms into sound energy.

Energy Path

  1. Battery supplies electrical energy.
  2. Closed switch completes the circuit.
  3. Wires carry electrical energy.
  4. Speaker or buzzer receives electrical energy.
  5. Speaker vibrates and produces sound energy.

Sound Signal Challenge

Which circuit will make the buzzer produce sound?

Correct answer: B. The closed conducting path allows electrical energy to reach the buzzer. The buzzer transforms electrical energy into sound energy.

Lab CER

Claim: Electrical energy can transform into sound energy in a complete circuit.

Evidence: The buzzer produces sound when the battery, conducting wires, switch, and buzzer form a complete circuit.

Reasoning: Electrical energy reaches the buzzer through the circuit. Parts inside the buzzer vibrate, producing sound energy.
