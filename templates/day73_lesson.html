👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — The Light Mission Map

➡️

Straight-Line Travel

Light travels straight until something changes its path.

Example: aligned openings
🪞

Reflection

Light bounces from a surface.

Example: mirror
💧

Refraction

Light changes direction when crossing between media.

Example: pencil in water
🌈

Visible Spectrum

White light contains ROYGBIV.

Example: prism / rainbow
👕

Reflection + Absorption

Reflected visible light determines the color we see.

Example: orange shirt
🪟

Light Through Materials

Materials may be transparent, translucent, or opaque.

Example: glass / wax paper / cardboard
Mission Strategy:

Don't just memorize the word.

Ask: “What evidence tells me what the light is doing?”
