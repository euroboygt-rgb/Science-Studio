Circuit Conductor Tester

Replace the test material, close the switch, and observe whether the bulb lights. If the bulb lights, the material is an electrical conductor. If it does not light, the material is an electrical insulator.

STAAR Connection

STAAR often asks students to decide whether a circuit is complete and whether a material allows electric current to flow. Look for the evidence: closed path, working power source, and a conductor in the path.
