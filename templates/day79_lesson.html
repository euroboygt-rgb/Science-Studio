🚨 Detective Warning

Real shadows do not have exactly the same direction and length everywhere on Earth.

Location, season, and time of year matter.

Our reliable evidence rules are: shadow generally points away from the Sun, and a higher Sun generally creates a shorter shadow.

🌎 What Causes the Daily Pattern?

Earth rotates west to east.

This makes the Sun appear to change position across our sky.

As the Sun's apparent position changes, shadows change too.

🤝 Guided Practice — Hide the Flashlight

Use a flashlight and a standing object.
  1. Create a long shadow but hide the flashlight from students' view.
  2. Ask students to infer where the flashlight must be.
  3. Create a short shadow.
  4. Ask students whether the light source is probably higher or lower.
  5. Switch the light source to the opposite side.
  6. Students explain how the shadow proves the source changed position.
