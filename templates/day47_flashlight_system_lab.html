Energy Path

  1. Battery stores chemical energy.
  2. Closed switch completes the circuit.
  3. Wires carry electrical energy.
  4. Bulb changes electrical energy into light and thermal energy.

Lab CER

Claim: A flashlight transforms energy when the circuit is complete.

Evidence: The battery, switch, wires, and bulb work together. The bulb shines and may feel warm.

Reasoning: Chemical energy in the battery changes to electrical energy in the circuit. The bulb changes electrical energy into light energy and thermal energy.
