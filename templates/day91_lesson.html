🔔 Bell Ringer — First or Later?

Imagine a clear container.

First you pour in a layer of sand.
Then you pour a layer of clay on top.
Then you pour a layer of small pebbles on top.

Which layer was deposited first? Which layer was deposited most recently?

Simple Undisturbed Model

Layer 1 was deposited. Then another layer settled on top. Then another. Then another.

BOTTOM = DEPOSITED FIRST
TOP = DEPOSITED LATER
⬇️

DEPOSITION

Adds new sediment on top of previously deposited material.

🗜️

COMPACTION

Weight from material above can squeeze lower sediments.

🧱

CEMENTATION

Minerals can bind compacted grains together.

🚨 Evidence Rule

Today we are using SIMPLE UNDISTURBED MODELS.

Do not make claims beyond what the model shows.
