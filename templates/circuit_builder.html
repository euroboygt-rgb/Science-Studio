How to Connect a Wire

  1. Add a wire.
  2. Grab either round wire end.
  3. Move it close to a terminal. The terminal will highlight.
  4. Release it and the wire will snap into place.
  5. Easy method: select a wire, then click a terminal to connect the next free end.
💡 Switch tip: Click the switch lever itself to open or close it.
↻ Rotation tip: Select a part and use Rotate 90°. Connected wires will follow the terminals.
