🎯 Learning Target

We will classify materials as transparent, translucent, or opaque based on how light passes through them.

🔔 Bell Ringer — Three Materials

A flashlight shines toward:
  • clear window glass,
  • wax paper,
  • cardboard.
Predict:
  1. Which material lets light pass through clearly?
  2. Which lets some light through but makes objects blurry?
  3. Which does not let light pass through?

👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — Three Ways Materials Interact With Light

🪟

Transparent

Most visible light passes through with little scattering.

You can see clearly through it.
🧻

Translucent

Some visible light passes through, but the light scatters.

Objects look blurry.
📦

Opaque

Visible light is not transmitted through the material.

You cannot see through it.
Light Detective Rule:

TRANSPARENT = passes through clearly
TRANSLUCENT = some passes through + scatters
OPAQUE = does not pass through
