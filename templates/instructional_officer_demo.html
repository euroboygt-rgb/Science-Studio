Science Studio Site Overview

This page is designed so an instructional leader can explore the 5th Grade Science Studio without needing a live walkthrough.

This app is a complete 1st 9 Weeks 5th Grade Science learning hub. It includes daily lessons, student and teacher views, interactive labs, vocabulary supports, STAAR-style review, digital assessment, and student reflection tools.

1 Student Dashboard

This is the student-friendly starting page. Students can open lessons, access labs, review games, assessments, and track progress.

This is the teacher command center. Teachers can launch any lesson, open teacher notes, access small-group reteach links, and use assessment/reflection tools.

Best Interactive Examples to Review

💧 Day 9: Graduated Cylinder Volume Simulator

Students pour liquid, read the mL measurement, drop objects into water, see displacement, and record volume data.

This is one of the strongest demo pieces. It visually shows the water level rising and connects directly to measurement, data, and evidence.
Open Day 9 Simulator

🚗 Day 36: Car on a Ramp Investigation

Students test a variable, collect distance data, graph results, and explain patterns.

Look for: fair-test thinking, variables, data table, graphing, and CER connection.
Open Ramp Investigation

🧭 Day 43: Science Stations Board

Students rotate through digital review stations with mini-simulators, voice demos, evidence tracking, and summary tools.

Look for: review station structure and student accountability through evidence tracking.
Open Science Stations

📝 Day 44: Digital Assessment

Students complete a STAAR-style assessment with score reporting and topic breakdown.

Look for: randomized questions, immediate feedback report, topic analysis, and teacher review mode.
Open Digital Assessment

🎯 Day 45: Reflection Tracker

Students use assessment data, notebook evidence, and confidence ratings to set a science goal.

Look for: assessment reflection, notebook checklist, goal setting, and printable reflection report.
Open Reflection Tracker

🔬 Full Interactive Lab Library

What This App Is Designed To Support

Student Learning

Students can interact with models, simulations, vocabulary visuals, review games, and assessment tools instead of only reading lesson text.

Teacher Planning

Teachers can open daily lesson plans, materials, vocabulary, teacher notes, lab activities, STAAR practice, and exit tickets.

STAAR Readiness

The app builds repeated practice with vocabulary, data, models, variables, evidence, graphing, and STAAR-style questions.
