🌊

4. COLLECTION

Water gathers in oceans, lakes, rivers, soil, and other places.

🌦️ How Does This Affect Weather?

Evaporation from the ocean adds water vapor to the atmosphere.

That moisture can later condense into clouds and may eventually become precipitation.

Sun + Ocean → Atmospheric Moisture → Clouds / Precipitation
🚨 Important Science Idea

Water does not disappear during evaporation.

It changes from liquid water into invisible water vapor.

The matter is still present.

  5. Collection: water gathers again.
