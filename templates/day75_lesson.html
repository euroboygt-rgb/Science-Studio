New → Full

📉

WANING

Full → New

⏱ Prediction Shortcut

~7 Days About ¼ of the cycle
~15 Days About ½ of the cycle
~22 Days About ¾ of the cycle
~29–30 Days About one full cycle
🚫 Important Misconception

Normal Moon phases are NOT caused by Earth's shadow.

Half of the Moon is always illuminated by the Sun. We see different portions of that illuminated half as the Moon moves around Earth.

Earth's shadow on the Moon happens during a lunar eclipse.
