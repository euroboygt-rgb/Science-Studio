🧠 Fossil Detective Rules

Two Rules We Must Remember

⬇️

RULE 1

OLDEST LAYER = BOTTOM

In the simple, undisturbed rock models we are using, lower layers were deposited before layers above them.

🐟🔎

RULE 2

FOSSILS = ENVIRONMENTAL EVIDENCE

Fossils can provide clues about organisms and environments that existed long ago.

🐟 + 🏜️ Fish fossil found in a desert today → Evidence that the area once had an aquatic environment.
🪨 W.E.D.C.C. Connection

👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — Fossils Are Clues

🔎 OBSERVATION

A fish fossil is found in rock.

→
🐟🌊 SCIENCE KNOWLEDGE

Fish live in aquatic environments.

→
🧠 INFERENCE

The area once had water.

→
🏜️ CHANGE

The environment is different today.

⬇️ Fossils + Layer Order

LAYER 4 deposited later
LAYER 3 🌿 leaf fossil
LAYER 2 🐚 shell fossil
LAYER 1 🐟 fish fossil — OLDEST LAYER
In this simple undisturbed model:

Layer 1 was deposited first.
Layer 4 was deposited later.

Therefore, the fossil in Layer 1 is found in an older layer than fossils in layers above it.
🚨 Don't Overclaim the Evidence

A fish fossil supports the conclusion that the area once had an aquatic environment.

It does NOT automatically tell us every detail about the ancient water body.

📓 Science Notebook — Fossil Detective Rules

RULE 1 ⬇️

Simple undisturbed model:
OLDEST LAYER AT THE BOTTOM

RULE 2 🦴

Fossils are EVIDENCE about past life.

RULE 3 🐟🌊

Aquatic fossils provide evidence of a past AQUATIC ENVIRONMENT.

RULE 4 🔄

Environments can CHANGE OVER TIME.

Write this evidence sentence:

A fish fossil found in a present-day desert is evidence that the area once had an aquatic environment.

🤝 Guided Practice — Evidence → Inference

Place a fish fossil in Layer 1, the bottom layer.

Ask:
  1. Which layer is oldest?
  2. What fossil evidence is present?
  3. What type of environment does a fish require?
  4. What changed?

"The evidence shows ______, so I infer ______."
