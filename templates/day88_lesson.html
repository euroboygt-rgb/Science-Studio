Four Rules Before Certification

☀️🌊 RULE 1

The Sun supplies energy. The ocean supplies water.

⬆️💨 RULE 2

Evaporation moves water into the atmosphere as invisible water vapor.

💨❄️☁️ RULE 3

Cooling moist air can support condensation and cloud formation.

☁️🌧️ RULE 4

A cloud does not guarantee precipitation.
