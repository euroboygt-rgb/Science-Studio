👨‍🏫 Mini Lesson — What We See vs. What Is Really Moving

👀

WHAT WE SEE

The Sun appears to move from east to west across the sky.

🌎

WHAT EARTH DOES

Earth rotates west to east on its axis.

🌅 SUNRISE Sun appears low in the EAST.
→
☀️ MORNING Sun appears higher.
→
🔆 SOLAR NOON Sun appears highest for the day.
→
☀️ AFTERNOON Sun appears lower.
→
🌇 SUNSET Sun appears low in the WEST.

⬛ Shadow Rule

🌅 LOW SUN

Longer shadow

🔆 HIGH SUN

Shorter shadow

🌇 LOW SUN

Longer shadow

A shadow extends generally away from the light source.

🚨 Apparent Motion

The Sun appears to cross our sky.

But the daily pattern happens because Earth is rotating.

Earth rotates west to east, making the Sun appear to move east to west.

📓 Science Notebook — Sun & Shadow Tracker

Draw a horizon.

Label: EAST on one side and WEST on the other.

Draw:
  1. Sunrise — Sun low in the east
  2. Solar noon — Sun highest
  3. Sunset — Sun low in the west
Add a standing object and show:
  • long morning shadow
  • short solar-noon shadow
  • long afternoon/evening shadow
Write: Earth's rotation causes the Sun's apparent daily motion.

🤝 Guided Practice — Flashlight Shadow Investigation

Use a flashlight and a standing object.
  1. Hold the flashlight low on one side.
  2. Observe the shadow.
  3. Raise the flashlight higher.
  4. Observe how the shadow shortens.
  5. Move the flashlight low on the opposite side.
  6. Observe how the shadow changes direction.
Ask: What pattern connects Sun height and shadow length?
